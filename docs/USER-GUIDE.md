# Sajawal POS 2.0 — Quick guide

## Making a sale
1. Open **Sell** (or press **F2**). Type a product name or scan its barcode. **Enter** adds it to the cart.
2. Change the quantity or price on the cart line if needed. A special price shows in orange; the ↻ button puts back the normal price.
3. For **udhaar**, press **F4** (or click *Walk-in customer*) and pick or add the customer. Their old balance is shown.
4. Press **Charge** (**F12**). Enter the money received.
   - **Exact** / 7,600 / 8,000 … are quick amounts.
   - **Bill + udhaar** receives this bill and all old udhaar together.
   - **Split payment** lets you take part in cash and part by EasyPaisa, JazzCash or card.
   - The box on the right shows the change to return and the customer's balance afterwards.
5. Choose **Receipt / A5 / A4 / No print** and press **Complete sale** (**Enter**).

**Customer watching the screen?** Press **F9**: all amounts are blurred. Hover over a line to see it.
**Customer still shopping?** Press **F8** (Park). Find the sale later under **Drafts**.

## Returns, cancellations and corrections
Open **Sales & Returns**, click the invoice, then:
- **Edit**: change items or quantities. The old version is kept in *Edit history*.
- **⋯ → Return items**: choose what comes back and how many. The refund is worked out for you. For an udhaar customer, the return reduces what they owe.
- **⋯ → Cancel whole sale**: everything comes back to stock and the bill is marked *Cancelled*.

## Udhaar (customer credit)
**Customers & Udhaar** shows who owes what. Open a customer to see the full **ledger** (every bill, payment and return with a running balance).
- **Receive payment** records money they pay; a receipt can print.
- **Statement** prints or saves a PDF to give to the customer.

## Stock
- **Inventory → Receive stock**: enter a supplier delivery (many products at once).
- **Adjust stock** on any product: *Add*, *Remove* (damaged, expired, tester) or *Set count* after counting the shelf. Every change is logged with the quantity.
- Click any product to see its full history: received, sold, returned, and to which customers.
- **Inventory → Import / Export**: bring products in from Excel. You check the columns and preview the rows before anything is saved.

## Reports
**Reports** works for any dates (click the date button):
- **Profit** by product, category, customer, invoice, day or month;
- **Day-end / cash**: what should be in the drawer;
- **Udhaar aging**: who has owed money the longest;
- **Stock value**;
- **Expenses**.

Every report has **Excel** and **Print / PDF**. Record rent, bills and salaries under **Expenses** to see true net profit.

## Owner PIN
**Settings → Security & locks** → *Set owner PIN*. Tick the pages (e.g. Reports, Dashboard) and actions (e.g. edit invoices, see profit) that need the PIN.
**Write down the recovery code**: it is the only way to reset a forgotten PIN. **Ctrl+L** locks immediately.

## Backups — please read
The app backs up automatically every day and when you close it. To protect against the computer failing:
**Settings → Backup & data → Second backup folder** → choose a USB drive or another disk.
To go back to a backup, use *Restore* in the same screen. A safety copy of the current data is made first.

## Printing
**Settings → Bills & printing**:
- choose the receipt printer and the A5 printer, then press **Test print**;
- the paper size is sent to the printer automatically;
- if the edges of an A5 bill are cut, increase *Page margin*;
- tick *Print straight to the chosen printer* to skip the print window.
